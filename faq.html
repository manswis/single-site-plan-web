FAQ & User Guide

Easy step-by-step instructions to find your property documents and complete your single plot plan.

Step 1

Property Records & Document Numbers

Enter the numbers printed on your government property certificates.

1. eKhata Property ID (ePID)

What it is: Your plot's 10-digit official digital ID number issued by BBMP.

Where to find it: Look at the top-right corner of your e-Khata document.

Sample Certificate Text
BBMP e-Khata Certificate
Property ID (ePID): 1509988776
Visit Karnataka e-Khata Portal ↗

2. BBMP Property Tax PID

What it is: The Tax ID number used when paying your yearly BBMP property tax.

Where to find it: Printed near the top of your yearly property tax receipt.

Sample Receipt Text
BBMP Property Tax Receipt
PID Number: 108-W0045-12
Visit BBMP Tax Portal ↗

3. 11E Survey Sketch Number

What it is: Government survey sketch number showing your plot's exact boundaries.

Where to find it: Printed on your Mojini 11E survey map from the survey department.

Sample Survey Map Text
Mojini 11E Survey Sketch
Sketch Ref: ADLR/11E/KR-7891/23-24
Visit Bhoomi Mojini Portal ↗

4. DC Conversion Order Number & Date

What it is: Government approval letter converting agricultural land for residential use.

Where to find it: Printed on your DC Conversion Order document.

Sample Order Text
District Commissioner Order
Order No: ALN(E)SR.97/07-08 (Dated: 12-04-2008)
Step 2

Property Location & Address

Enter where your plot is located in Bengaluru.

1. 198 BBMP Ward Auto-Suggest Directory

What it is: Select your BBMP administrative zone and search through all 198 BBMP wards with instant locality & landmark suggestions.

Where to find it: Check your BBMP Property Tax receipt or enter your locality name (e.g., Malleshwaram, Indiranagar, Whitefield).

2. Interactive Map Pin & GPS Precision

What it is: Pinpoint your site coordinates on an interactive satellite/street map with customizable zoom levels (14–18) for the locational Key Plan.

Where to find it: Tap 'Pick on Map' or use 'Locate Me' to automatically capture your GPS latitude and longitude coordinates.

3. Registered Site Address

What it is: Full site address including survey number, site number, layout name, and cross road.

Where to find it: Printed in Schedule 'A' or Schedule 'B' of your registered Sale Deed.

Step 3

Plot Size & Road Width

Enter your plot measurements and road details.

1. Regular vs Irregular Plot Boundaries

What it is: Choose Regular (rectangular/square) for standard plots or Irregular (odd site) to enter 4 independent side lengths.

Where to find it: Refer to the dimensions column in your Sale Deed schedule or 11E survey sketch.

2. Karnataka Land Measure Area Converter

What it is: Convert seamlessly across 9 statutory Karnataka land units (Guntha, Ankanam, Square Yards, Gajam, Bigha, Cents, Ares) into Square Feet.

Where to find it: Tap the land converter icon next to Plot Area or choose standard Bangalore plot presets (30×40, 40×60, etc.).

3. Smart Numeric Auto-Tabbing (Feet ➔ Inches)

What it is: Rapid typing support: entering two digits or typing '.' / Space / Enter automatically advances focus from feet to inches.

Where to find it: Available across all 15+ boundary and setback dimension fields.

Step 4

Building Plan & Open Spaces (Setbacks)

Set your building floors and required open spaces.

1. Building Type & Number of Floors

What it is: Select Vacant Plot, Ground Floor, Stilt+Ground, or G+1 to G+4 to calculate total built-up area and structural footprint.

Where to find it: Check your approved architectural building plan or existing on-site construction.

2. Automated 2026 Table 8 & 9 Setback Compliance

What it is: Auto-computes mandatory Front, Rear, Left, and Right setbacks based on 2026 Karnataka Gazetted metric thresholds (≤60 sqm, 60–150 sqm, 150–250 sqm, 250–4000 sqm), supporting single-side zero setback tolerances on small plots and live FAR eligibility.

Where to find it: Based on the 2026 Karnataka Gazette Notification (UDD 235 MNJ 2025(E)) and BBMP Building Bye-Laws.

3. Feasibility & Overlap Prevention

What it is: Instant architectural checks ensure building footprint fits safely within plot boundaries after deducting setbacks.

Where to find it: Automatic warning banner alerts if building dimensions exceed available plot space.

Step 5

Deed DNA & Boundary Descriptions

Enter surrounding boundaries as written in your Sale Deed schedule.

1. Deed DNA & Surrounding Boundaries

What it is: Enter neighboring properties, public roads, private sites, or vacant land for North, South, East, and West boundaries.

Where to find it: Refer to the 'Boundaries' / 'ಚಕ್ಕುಬಂದಿ' paragraph in the Schedule of your registered Sale Deed.

Step 6

Constraints & Municipal Compliance

Check road widening, buffer zones, and signature attachments.

1. Road Widening & Stormwater Drain Buffers

What it is: Show statutory road widening reservations or Rajakaluve drain buffer corridors directly on the drawing sheet.

Where to find it: Indicated in BBMP Master Plan notifications or local town planning zonal maps.

2. Owner Signature & Architect Seal Crop Tool

What it is: Upload photos of signatures or seals. Use the interactive pan/zoom crop tool with chromaticity contrast auto-cleaning to remove grey paper background.

Where to find it: Upload directly from your phone camera or computer image files.

Step 7

Instant Review & PDF Download

Review your drawing and download the 2-page Sakala PDF package.

1. Interactive Review & Legal Consent Gating

What it is: Structured summary accordion lets you inspect all data and jump back to edit any step before plan generation.

Where to find it: Step 7 Review hub with statutory zero-liability legal consent confirmation.

2. Consolidated 2-Page Sakala PDF Package & Portability

What it is: Generates a 2-page package (Page 1: Single Plot Layout Plan + Page 2: Line & Colour Specifications). Includes 'Save Project' / 'Open Project' .json portability.

Where to find it: Direct client-side generation ready for instant printing or Sakala e-Khata upload.

What is BBMP B-to-A Khata conversion and why do I need a site plan? +
B-Khata is a temporary tax entry for unapproved properties. A-Khata is the official permanent ownership certificate issued by BBMP. To get A-Khata, BBMP requires a clean single plot layout plan showing your plot boundaries, road width, and setbacks.
Where can I get my eKhata ID (ePID)? +
You can check or download your eKhata ID from the official Karnataka e-Khata portal (bbmpekhata.karnataka.gov.in). It is a 10-digit number printed on the top-right corner of your e-Khata letter.
What is an 11E Survey Sketch? +
An 11E Sketch is an official survey sketch map issued by the government land records department (ADLR) via the Bhoomi Mojini system. It confirms the exact physical shape and measurements of your plot.
How are open spaces (setbacks) calculated? +
Setbacks are open spaces you must leave on the front, back, and sides of your building. Our app automatically calculates the required setbacks based on your plot size and road width following BBMP master plan regulations.
What if my plot has 4 unequal or slanted sides? +
On Step 3, check the "Irregular Plot" box. You can then enter all 4 side lengths (North, South, East, West) individually. The system will draw the plot shape accurately.
Is my property data safe and private? +
Yes, 100%. All your data is saved and processed inside your web browser. No property details or personal information are sent or saved on external servers.
Does my final plan need an architect's signature? +
Yes. The generated PDF comes with standard signature and seal boxes for your licensed architect or engineer to sign before submitting to BBMP.
Can I edit my details if I make a mistake? +
Yes. On Step 7 (Summary), you can click the "Edit" button next to any section to go back and update your information anytime.
