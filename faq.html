FAQ & Complete User Guide

Step-by-step instructions to find official Karnataka revenue records, understand every form field, and prepare your BBMP Sakala B-to-A Khata plan.

Step 1

🏛️ Revenue Records & Identifiers

Enter official identifiers required for BBMP e-Khata validation and B-to-A Khata title conversion.

1. eKhata ID (ePID)

What it is: A unique 10-digit digital property identification number assigned by BBMP / Sakala e-Khata portal.

Where to find it: Printed on top right of your draft or final e-Khata certificate (bbmpekhata.karnataka.gov.in).

Official Document Snippet
BBMP e-Khata Certificate
e-Khata Property ID (ePID): 1509988776
🔗 Visit Karnataka e-Khata Portal ↗

2. BBMP PID Number

What it is: Property Identification Number used for annual SAS Property Tax payments in Bengaluru.

Where to find it: Printed on your annual SAS Property Tax receipt or tax khata record.

SAS Tax Receipt Snippet
BBMP Property Tax Receipt
PID No: 108-W0045-12
🔗 BBMP Tax Receipt Portal ↗

3. ADLR 11E Survey Sketch No

What it is: Pre-mutation survey sketch reference issued by the Assistant Director of Land Records (ADLR).

Where to find it: Issued via Bhoomi Mojini portal (e.g. ADLR/11E/KR-7891/2023-24).

Mojini 11E Sketch Snippet
Mojini 11E Survey Sketch
Sketch Ref: ADLR/11E/KR-7891/23-24
🔗 Bhoomi Mojini Portal ↗

4. DC Conversion Order No & Date

What it is: Land conversion order from agricultural to non-agricultural residential use under KLR Act Sec 95.

Where to find it: Printed on your official DC Conversion Order issued by DC, Bengaluru Urban District.

DC Order Snippet
District Commissioner Order
Order No: ALN(E)SR.97/07-08 (Dt: 12-04-2008)
Step 2

📍 Property Location & Administration

Select administrative details to align your site plan with BBMP zonal jurisdiction.

1. BBMP Zone & Ward Details

Select your administrative zone (e.g. East, West, South, Mahadevapura, Bommanahalli, Yelahanka, Dasarahalli, Rajarajeshwarinagar) and enter official Ward Number & Name (e.g. Ward #45, Malleshwaram).

2. Complete Site Address

Enter the full physical site location matching your registered deed (Door No, Cross, Main Road, Locality, Pincode).

Step 3

📐 Plot Measurements & Geometry

Input precise physical site measurements to generate accurate CAD vector geometry.

1. Regular vs Irregular Plot Mode

Regular Site: For rectangular or square plots, enter North-South and East-West dimensions.

Irregular Site: For non-rectangular sites, check "Irregular Site" to enter 4 independent side lengths (North, South, East, West).

2. Road Width & Facing Direction

Select the direction of the front main road (North, South, East, West) and enter the road width in feet and inches (e.g. 30 ft 0 in). Road width determines minimum setback rules under BBMP RMP-2015 bye-laws.

Step 4

🏗️ Structure & Building Setbacks

Define floor counts and setback clearances compliant with municipal zoning bye-laws.

1. Building Type & Floors Count

Choose building category (Residential, Commercial, Mixed Use) and floor structure (Vacant Plot, Stilt+Ground, G+1 to G+4).

2. Auto-Calculated Setbacks (RMP-2015)

Setbacks (Front, Rear, Left, Right) are automatically computed based on plot size and road width according to BBMP RMP-2015 bye-laws. You may adjust values manually if required.

3. Footprint Alignment Direction

Select whether the building long side runs horizontally (along North-South span) or vertically (along East-West span) to prevent footprint boundary overflow.

Step 5 & 6

📜 Deed DNA & Special Constraints

Define abutting site boundaries and Master Plan road widening or buffer zone constraints.

1. Schedule of Property Boundaries

From your Registered Sale Deed Schedule, select what abuts each cardinal side (Public Road, Private Property, Government Land, Stormwater Drain).

2. Road Widening & Nala Buffer Zones

If your property is affected by Master Plan road widening or a Nala/Lake buffer, toggle the constraint checkbox to render the official hatched zone on the plan sheet.

❓ What is BBMP B-to-A Khata conversion and why is a Single Plot Layout Plan required? +
B-Khata is a temporary property tax register entry for unauthorized layouts. A-Khata is the official ownership khata certificate issued by BBMP for DC-converted properties with valid single plot layout plans. The single plot layout plan proves site dimensions, road access, and setback compliance under KTCP Act Sec 17.
❓ Where do I get my official eKhata ID (ePID)? +
You can retrieve or verify your eKhata ID (ePID) from the official Karnataka e-Khata portal (bbmpekhata.karnataka.gov.in). It is a 10-digit number printed on top right of your draft or final e-Khata certificate.
❓ What is an ADLR 11E Survey Sketch and why is it needed? +
An ADLR 11E Sketch is a pre-mutation survey map issued by the Assistant Director of Land Records via the Bhoomi Mojini portal. It establishes official revenue survey boundaries for single plot layout approval.
❓ How are building setbacks calculated on the workbench? +
Setbacks are dynamically calculated according to BBMP Revised Master Plan (RMP-2015) bye-laws based on abutting road width and plot depth. The system allocates minimum mandatory front, rear, left, and right setbacks while allowing manual adjustment.
❓ How does the software handle irregular or 4-side unequal plots? +
In Step 3, check "Irregular / Odd Shaped Site" to enter 4 independent side measurements (North, South, East, West). The CAD engine computes non-rectangular quadrilateral geometry and bounds setbacks against the shortest side to prevent boundary overflow.
❓ Is my personal or property data uploaded to any cloud server? +
100% NO! All processing, SVG vector rendering, draft auto-persistence, and PDF export occur locally inside your browser using HTML5 LocalStorage and Canvas. Zero property records or personal data are ever uploaded to any external server.
❓ Do I still need a licensed architect or engineer signature on the plan? +
Yes. The generated PDF includes official COA/BBMP registration signature and seal blocks ready for your licensed architect, chartered engineer, or BBMP supervisor to review, sign, and seal before municipal submission.
❓ What is the Page 2 Legend & Specifications Sheet? +
Page 2 is an optional technical legend sheet containing official line style specifications, hatch pattern codes, RMP-2015 setback matrices, and Sakala B-to-A Khata mandatory document checklists.
❓ Can I edit my inputs after reviewing the summary in Step 7? +
Yes! On Step 7, each summary card features a prominent "Edit" button that takes you directly back to that wizard step, focuses the target field, and highlights it for fast modification.
