FAQ & User Guide

Easy step-by-step instructions to find your property documents and complete your single plot plan.

Step 1

Property Records & Document Numbers

Enter the numbers printed on your government property certificates.

1. eKhata Property ID (ePID)

What it is: Your plot's 10-digit official digital ID number issued by BBMP.

Where to find it: Look at the top-right corner of your e-Khata document.

Sample Certificate Text
BBMP e-Khata Certificate
Property ID (ePID): 1509988776
Visit Karnataka e-Khata Portal ↗

2. BBMP Property Tax PID

What it is: The Tax ID number used when paying your yearly BBMP property tax.

Where to find it: Printed near the top of your yearly property tax receipt.

Sample Receipt Text
BBMP Property Tax Receipt
PID Number: 108-W0045-12
Visit BBMP Tax Portal ↗

3. 11E Survey Sketch Number

What it is: Government survey sketch number showing your plot's exact boundaries.

Where to find it: Printed on your Mojini 11E survey map from the survey department.

Sample Survey Map Text
Mojini 11E Survey Sketch
Sketch Ref: ADLR/11E/KR-7891/23-24
Visit Bhoomi Mojini Portal ↗

4. DC Conversion Order Number & Date

What it is: Government approval letter converting agricultural land for residential use.

Where to find it: Printed on your DC Conversion Order document.

Sample Order Text
District Commissioner Order
Order No: ALN(E)SR.97/07-08 (Dated: 12-04-2008)
Step 2

Property Location & Address

Enter where your plot is located in Bengaluru.

1. BBMP Zone & Ward

Select your BBMP zone (like East, West, South, or Mahadevapura) and type your Ward number and name (like Ward 45, Malleshwaram).

2. Site Address

Type your full property address as written on your registered Sale Deed.

Step 3

Plot Size & Road Width

Enter your plot measurements and road details.

1. Regular vs Irregular Plot

Regular Plot: Select this if your site is a simple rectangle or square.

Irregular Plot: Check this if your plot has 4 unequal or slanted sides so you can enter each side length separately.

2. Front Road Width & Direction

Choose which direction your main road faces (North, South, East, or West) and enter the road width in feet (for example, 30 feet).

Step 4

Building Plan & Open Spaces (Setbacks)

Set your building floors and required open spaces.

1. Building Type & Floors

Choose whether your site is a vacant plot or has a building (like Ground Floor, G+1, G+2, etc.).

2. Automatic Setbacks

Setbacks are the required open spaces around your building. The software automatically calculates the required open space based on BBMP rules.

3. Building Direction

Choose whether your building length runs side-to-side or front-to-back so the drawing fits nicely on your plot.

Step 5 & 6

Boundaries & Road Widening

Enter neighboring details and special road or drain rules.

1. Neighboring Boundaries

Look at the "Schedule" section of your Sale Deed and select what is on each side of your plot (Road, Private Site, or Vacant Land).

2. Road Widening or Drain Buffer

If the government has reserved space for road widening or a stormwater drain next to your site, turn on this option to show it on your drawing.

What is BBMP B-to-A Khata conversion and why do I need a site plan? +
B-Khata is a temporary tax entry for unapproved properties. A-Khata is the official permanent ownership certificate issued by BBMP. To get A-Khata, BBMP requires a clean single plot layout plan showing your plot boundaries, road width, and setbacks.
Where can I get my eKhata ID (ePID)? +
You can check or download your eKhata ID from the official Karnataka e-Khata portal (bbmpekhata.karnataka.gov.in). It is a 10-digit number printed on the top-right corner of your e-Khata letter.
What is an 11E Survey Sketch? +
An 11E Sketch is an official survey sketch map issued by the government land records department (ADLR) via the Bhoomi Mojini system. It confirms the exact physical shape and measurements of your plot.
How are open spaces (setbacks) calculated? +
Setbacks are open spaces you must leave on the front, back, and sides of your building. Our app automatically calculates the required setbacks based on your plot size and road width following BBMP master plan regulations.
What if my plot has 4 unequal or slanted sides? +
On Step 3, check the "Irregular Plot" box. You can then enter all 4 side lengths (North, South, East, West) individually. The system will draw the plot shape accurately.
Is my property data safe and private? +
Yes, 100%. All your data is saved and processed inside your web browser. No property details or personal information are sent or saved on external servers.
Does my final plan need an architect's signature? +
Yes. The generated PDF comes with standard signature and seal boxes for your licensed architect or engineer to sign before submitting to BBMP.
Can I edit my details if I make a mistake? +
Yes. On Step 7 (Summary), you can click the "Edit" button next to any section to go back and update your information anytime.
